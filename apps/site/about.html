Why we built it.

Running buses means juggling seats, cash, crews, vehicles and people, usually across paper, phones and spreadsheets. We wanted one system where all of that is connected and simple enough for a busy counter on a Monday morning.

So we built NTS Transport: one platform for passengers, staff and owners that works on a phone and carries each company’s own brand.
