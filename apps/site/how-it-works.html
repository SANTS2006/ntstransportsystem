Home / How it works

Live in a few simple steps.

No installation and no servers to look after. You sign in on the web and your company is ready to set up.

1. Register your company

We register your company on the platform and make you its first administrator. You get a sign-in with a temporary password to change at first login.

2. Add your brand

Upload your logo, choose your colours and name. Pages, tickets and emails now look like yours, on your own web address.

3. Build the network

Add terminals, routes, stops and fares, vehicles and seat layouts.

4. Bring in your people

Create roles, add staff or recruit them through the job board. Everyone gets only the access their role allows.

5. Publish schedules

Weekly schedules create trips. Set blackout days, assign crew and vehicles.

6. Start selling

Passengers book online and agents sell at the desk. Tickets, payments and notices flow automatically.

A day on the system

What happens between 05:00 and midnight.

Early morning

Dispatch checks the board, confirms crews and swaps a bus that failed inspection. Passengers on that trip get a notice.

Boarding

The conductor scans each ticket with a phone. Invalid or used tickets are refused on the spot.

Daytime sales

Agents sell remaining seats and online bookings keep arriving. Seats update live everywhere.

Evening

Agents cash up, finance reviews the day and the owner reads the reports. Everything is logged.
