The journey

From route to revenue, step by step.

01

Set up your network

Add terminals, routes with stops and fares, vehicles and seat layouts. Reuse them for every trip.

02

Schedule trips

Weekly schedules create trips automatically, with a calendar and blackout days for holidays and breakdowns.

03

Sell seats

Passengers book on your page. Agents sell at the desk. Payment confirms the seat and sends the ticket.

04

Run the day

Dispatch assigns the crew, boarding scans tickets, and delays or bus swaps reach passengers at once.

05

Look after people and cash

Cash-up, refunds and reports for finance. Recruitment, hiring and salaries for HR. Every action on record.

Made for every role

Each person sees exactly what their job needs.

Roles decide what shows in the menu and what the server allows. Staff who wear several hats can switch between them.

Passengers

Search trips, pick seats, pay, keep tickets on the phone, rate the ride and raise a complaint with photos or a voice note.

Dispatch and drivers

Dispatch builds the day and assigns crew with licence and rest rules checked. Drivers see their trips and scan tickets.

Sales and finance

Agents sell and take payment at the desk. Finance cashes up, refunds, reads reports and manages salaries.

Owners and HR

Owners shape roles and branding. HR runs recruitment, hiring and staff records. Auditors read the activity log.

Built for the phone

Tickets that travel in your pocket.

The whole system installs like an app and keeps working on a weak connection. Passengers carry their tickets, staff scan with the camera, and nothing needs a separate download.

  • Installable on Android and iPhone, with an offline page
  • Camera scanning at boarding, with a beep for the answer
  • Light and dark themes that follow the phone
