How we handle personal data.

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain language, how NTS Digital Solutions handles personal information on this website and in the NTS Transport system.

This website

This website does not use accounts and does not store what you type in the contact form. The form opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message, and what happens next is governed by those services.

The NTS Transport system

Transport companies that use the system are responsible for the passenger, staff and applicant information they collect. We process it on their behalf to provide the service, keep each company’s data separate and do not sell it.

Security

Access is limited by role, passwords are stored as hashes, files are private and important actions are logged.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or remove personal information, contact the company you dealt with, or write to us at hello@ntsdigitalsolutions.com.
