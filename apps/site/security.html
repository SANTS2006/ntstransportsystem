Your data stays yours.

Transport companies hold passenger, payment and staff information. We treat protecting it as part of the product, not an add-on.

Isolation

One company can never read another’s data.

Every record belongs to a company and the database itself enforces it with row-level security. Even a mistake in the application cannot cross that line.

Company Asees A
Company Bsees B

Roles and permissions

Access is decided on the server. Nobody can hand out permissions they do not hold themselves.

Two-step sign-in

Optional authenticator app codes with recovery codes, and Google sign-in for those who prefer it.

Private files

CVs, licence photos and attachments are never public. They open only through short-lived links, and staff access is logged.

Audit trail

Sign-ins, role changes, hires, refunds and more are written to a log that cannot be edited.

Safe by design

Rate limits on sensitive actions, safe retries so nothing is charged twice, and checks at the database as well as the screen.

Backups

Platform administrators can take and restore backups, and maintenance mode shows a clear notice to everyone.

Privacy

Personal data is used for the journey, nothing else.

Applicant details are visible only to the company’s HR team. Passwords are stored as hashes and first passwords must be changed at first sign-in. Read the privacy policy for the details.
